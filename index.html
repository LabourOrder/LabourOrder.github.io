Profile

Experimental physicist and microelectronics R&D researcher with end-to-end experience in thin-film device fabrication, electrical characterization, synchrotron-based spectroscopy (HAXPES, DAFS), data analysis, and physics-based modeling. My research focuses on HfO₂–ZrO₂ ferroelectric devices, reliability, electrically active defects, and buried interfaces. I develop custom experimental setups, automated measurement workflows, and operando synchrotron experiments.
